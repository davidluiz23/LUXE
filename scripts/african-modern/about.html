ALKEBULAN is an exploration of African identity through everyday clothing. Art, memory, rhythm, and personal style meet on a canvas you can wear.

We begin with three pieces: Ijele, Durbar, and Dùn Dùn. Each carries its own visual language. You give it your own life.
