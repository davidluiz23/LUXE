## Print rhythm — exact generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original contemporary abstract geometric relief-print texture for ALKEBULAN, a Nigerian fashion brand. Landscape 1536 by 1024 flat artwork scan. Warm ivory paper with a loose, elegant rhythm of charcoal-black thick angular fragments, open broken chevrons, asymmetric stair-step turns, short parallel lines, broad irregular arcs and dotted clusters. The visual language is contemporary West African graphic-poster energy and tactile linocut ink, without reproducing any existing art, cultural glyph, specific ceremonial symbol, or sacred motif. Marks feel hand-cut, slightly worn and imperfect, with fine ink grain. Broad confident forms, generous breathing space: at least 65 percent ivory negative space, several independent clusters, avoid a dense wallpaper. Compose like abstract printmaking, no recognizable object, no faces, no masks, no animals, no map, no letters, no text, no logo, no watermark, no border, no room, no clothing, no website mockup. Black and warm ivory only. This asset is a material texture for a separately implemented interactive orange contour-flow shader. The resulting artwork must be original and visually different from existing pattern designs.

## Woven current — exact generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an ORIGINAL sophisticated contemporary West African textile-inspired abstract fine-art painting, landscape 1536 by 1024. This will become a continuous liquid interactive page background for an African clothing brand. A beautifully restrained composition with large airy areas of warm off-white natural cotton, flowing broad indigo and dusty blue dyed bands, a few muted burnt-sienna and ochre pigment areas. Build the flowing bands from rhythmic narrow woven cotton strips, with subtle resist-dye dotted lines, original irregular broken arcs and hand-painted short hatch marks within selected bands. Visual qualities: dyed fiber, tactile layered threads, dye bleeding into natural linen, gently undulating textile surfaces, elegant negative space. Large sweeping indigo textile arch enters from upper right; a contrasting sienna arc enters lower left, smaller deep blue detail lower right. At least 55 percent clean warm ivory negative space through center and upper left. Restrained saturation, sophisticated but clearly visible marks, softer and more material than poster graphics. Contemporary abstract textile art as a real physical surface photographed straight-on; flat composition, not a framed object. Do not copy any existing artwork or named artist. No actual sacred, ceremonial, religious or written symbols. No faces, masks, people, letters, logos, maps, text, watermark, website mockup, garment, or photographic room. No neon, no generic gradient blobs, no smooth marbling. A unique fine-art cloth composition in indigo, natural linen, clay and ochre.
