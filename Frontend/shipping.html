From order confirmation to waybill tracking, every important update stays attached to your ALKEBULAN order.

How fulfilment works

  1. Checkout saves the order to your account and the ALKEBULAN admin console.
  2. The team confirms availability, delivery details and the estimated arrival window.
  3. After dispatch, a waybill or tracking link can be added to your order.
  4. You can follow the status in My Account and, with consent, through WhatsApp updates.

Delivery estimates

Delivery time depends on the destination, item availability and the selected courier. The estimate shown on your order is provided after fulfilment review and is not a guaranteed delivery appointment. Courier delays, public holidays, weather and incomplete addresses can affect arrival.

Address accuracy

Check the recipient name, phone number, street address, city and state before placing an order. Contact ALKEBULAN immediately if something is wrong; an address may be impossible to change after dispatch.

Waybill and delivery questions

Open Order History in My Account for the latest status. If a parcel appears delayed, contact ALKEBULAN with the order number so the team can check with the courier.
