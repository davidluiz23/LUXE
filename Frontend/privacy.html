Your Information

Privacy Notice

A plain-language overview of the information used to operate your ALKEBULAN account and fulfil orders.

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Information ALKEBULAN uses

ALKEBULAN processes information you provide when creating an account, maintaining a profile, subscribing to updates, contacting support or placing an order. This can include your name, email address, verified WhatsApp number, delivery address, ordered items and fulfilment history. For account security and fraud investigation, authentication records may also include sign-in time, IP address, device or browser information and the type of sign-in event.

Store traffic is measured using a random browser ID saved in local storage. Live activity records the page being viewed; daily history keeps the browser ID and visit date to count returning browsers once per day. Daily history does not store your name, email address, account ID, IP address or device fingerprint. Store administrators can view aggregate visitor and account statistics.

Why it is used

  • To authenticate and protect your account.
  • To save, confirm, dispatch and support your orders.
  • To send account, order and delivery notifications you have requested.
  • To maintain store security, prevent abuse and meet operational obligations.

Service providers

ALKEBULAN uses service providers to operate parts of the store. Supabase supports account and order storage; Cloudinary may host product media; email delivery providers may send requested account or store messages; WhatsApp may deliver messages you consent to receive; a payment processor such as Paystack may handle online payments when enabled; and delivery partners receive the information necessary to deliver a parcel.

ALKEBULAN does not store complete card details. When online payment is available, sensitive payment details are entered on the payment processor's secure service. ALKEBULAN may retain the payment provider, payment channel and a limited descriptor such as card brand and last four digits for support and fraud review.

Browser storage and security

The site may use browser storage for session continuity, cart and wishlist state, and interface preferences. Access controls restrict customers to their own protected records, while administrative actions require an authorised admin account. Authorised administrators may review account security history when needed for customer support, fraud prevention or abuse investigation. Sensitive admin decisions are recorded in an audit trail.

Your choices

You can decline WhatsApp order updates, update profile information, or contact ALKEBULAN about an account or privacy question. Some order information may need to be retained for fulfilment, fraud prevention, accounting or legal reasons.

Privacy contact

For questions or requests concerning your information, email hello@alkebulan.com and include enough account information for ALKEBULAN to verify the request securely.

Requests are handled subject to identity verification, applicable law, and records ALKEBULAN must retain for legitimate operational or legal purposes.
