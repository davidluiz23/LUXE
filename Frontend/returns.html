Customer Care

Returns & Exchanges

A straightforward process for reporting an issue or requesting an eligible return.

FAQReturnsShippingPrivacyTerms

Contact us before sending anything back

Request an eligible return within 30 calendar days of delivery. Message ALKEBULAN with your order number and the reason for the request. The team will confirm eligibility and provide the correct return instructions. Unapproved parcels may not be identifiable when received.

Return condition

Unless an item arrived damaged, incorrect or defective, it should be unworn, unwashed and returned with its original tags and packaging. Items showing wear, alteration, fragrance, stains or damage after delivery may not qualify.

Damaged or incorrect orders

Contact us promptly after delivery and include clear photographs of the item, packaging and waybill. ALKEBULAN will review the issue and explain the available replacement, exchange or refund route.

Exceptions

Personalised items, hygiene-sensitive goods after their seal is opened, and items clearly identified as final sale may not be returnable unless they arrived defective or your applicable consumer rights require otherwise.

How to request help

  1. Open your order in My Account and note the order number.
  2. Contact hello@alkebulan.com or use the contact page.
  3. Wait for approval and return instructions before dispatching the item.
  4. Keep the return waybill or delivery receipt until the request is completed.

This policy does not limit any non-waivable consumer rights that apply to your purchase.
