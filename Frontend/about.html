We wear where
we come from.

ALKEBULAN brings clothing and artwork together. For us, African heritage is a point of connection between people, memory, and visual expression. It informs the work we make and the stories we carry forward.

Our perspective is contemporary. Our expression is personal. And the next chapter belongs to the person wearing it.
